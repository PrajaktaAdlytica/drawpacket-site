What this preview does.

Effective for this concept preview: 15 October 2026.

01

Forms

Values remain in the current page’s memory. No form submission endpoint or contact list is connected. Leaving or reloading the page clears those fields.

02

Demo

All seeded records are synthetic. Demo decisions remain local. An export is created only when you explicitly download it.

03

Analytics

Interaction events are dispatched locally in the browser. No analytics service or tracking cookie is configured. Events exclude contact details and raw financial records.

04

Assets and links

Fonts and brand assets are self-hosted. Following an external reference opens that provider’s site, where its own privacy practices apply.
