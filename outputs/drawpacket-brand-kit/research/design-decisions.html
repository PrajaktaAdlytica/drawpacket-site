Hover research translated into an original vocabulary

The 12 treatments in the kit are proposed design patterns, not claims about animations observed on competitor sites. Surface wipe, split shutter, type flip, arrow exchange, editorial underline, border emphasis, surface exchange, index emphasis, icon exchange, directional nudge, disclosure state, and caption lift each have a narrow purpose. Use at most three on a given page.

For DrawPacket, a directional nudge is the everyday navigation default; a wipe is the primary CTA; an editorial underline is the quiet text link. Flip and shutter belong to marketing chapters. Review controls remain visually stable.

Type selection

Bodoni Moda gives the short editorial headlines contrast and personality. DM Sans supports readable operational language. IBM Plex Mono separates IDs and numerical evidence. Source Serif 4 supplies the outlined logo wordmark. These are deliberate licensed font choices, not an assertion that the generated concept image used those exact fonts.

  • Background images: three original AI-generated abstract material studies created for this kit. They are not documentary photographs. PNG masters and optimized WebP copies are included.
  • Textures and diagrams: original SVG constructions. The reviewer figure is an original vector line illustration. All product entities and figures are synthetic.

Background image briefs

  1. Chalk architecture: warm chalk architectural arches, staggered heights and a gentle diagonal seam, restrained shadows, no symbols or text, right-weighted composition and clear left-side negative space.
  2. Lilac evidence: translucent lilac sheets and glass-like planes, offset edges, soft studio light, clear left side, restrained high-end editorial material study without literal UI.
  3. Oxblood boundary: deep burgundy architectural material and a precise seam, quiet controlled light, right-weighted form, no glow, no text, no technology cliché.

These are design prompt summaries. The source PNGs preserve the generated results; motion is applied in the browser rather than baked into an AI-generated video.

Research scope and remaining work

Perplexity’s high research preset was used for synthesis, followed by checks of relevant primary documentation and official brand/font assets. The raw research response is retained in research-response.json. Design choices are the kit’s recommendations, not claims of market validation.

The kit has no production integration, privacy backend, legal approval, demand proof, or paid customers. The production website and deterministic product demo still require their own implementation and validation. No MP4 files are included; motion deliverables are browser CSS/SVG sequences.
