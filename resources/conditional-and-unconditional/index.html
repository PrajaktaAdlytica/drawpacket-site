02Waiver reading guide

Two distinctions to preserve.

California CSLB guidance is an example only. The project’s state, contract and approved form set govern the actual review.

01

Conditional

Release depends on the stated payment conditions. A signature alone does not establish that payment cleared.

02

Unconditional

The California example acknowledges payment receipt. Do not infer receipt from document arrival.

03

Progress / final

Payment stage matters too. A form for one stage is not automatically appropriate for another.
