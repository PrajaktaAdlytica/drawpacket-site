What stays with the human.

The following responsibilities cannot be delegated to a scroll animation or a suggested state.

01

Forms and jurisdiction

GC and counsel approve state-specific waiver forms and contract interpretation.

02

Evidence acceptance

The reviewer confirms source versions, assumptions and reasons.

03

04

Release authority
